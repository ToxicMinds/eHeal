# AppHeader

The proof-of-concept band, then the header: the wordmark, who is signed in, and "Sign out". On every signed-in screen.

- The band stays until real patients are allowed (spec Gate R). Its words do not change: "Development proof of concept. Invented data only."
- The email truncates on narrow screens; "Sign out" never does.
- The wordmark links to the consultation list.

The consumer provides: the signed-in email and the sign-out action.
